## The Brief

Dougie's Discs is built for vinyl enthusiasts who value the tangible culture of physical music. Rather than feeling like a bland generic webshop, the site is designed as an intimate, editorial crate-digging experience.

## The Architecture

- **Discogs & Release Sync** — Structured metadata schema bringing album artwork, tracklists, matrix numbers, and release variants directly into the browsing experience.
- **Audio Previews** — Ambient embedded snippet player allowing visitors to listen to selected cuts while reading album essays.
- **Collector Wishlists** — Fast client-side bookmarking and want-list tracking for local stock notifications.
