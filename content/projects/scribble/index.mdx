## The idea

The scattered Notepad files, unsent emails, Teams messages, and sticky
notes that pile up during a working day all share one problem: they
demand you decide what a thought *is* before you're allowed to write
it down. Scribble removes that demand — capture first, organise later,
if at all.

## What's inside

A system-tray app for Windows, summoned with a global hotkey and
dismissed with Escape, that reopens exactly as it was left. Notes,
checklists, pen input, and dropped files land on one continuous
surface with automatic timestamps and full-text search. A local
rules-based organiser can suggest groupings — actions, decisions,
questions, people — but every suggestion is reviewed, and the original
notes are never silently rewritten.

## Why it matters to me

Built as a native Rust/Tauri app deliberately, not a web wrapper — a
genuinely local, private tool for a genuinely private use case: the
messy first-draft thinking that happens before anything is ready to go
in a real system.
