## The idea

Every household loses track of what it's actually paying for — a
streaming trial that never got cancelled, two AI subscriptions doing
the same job, a broadband contract that rolled onto a worse rate.
Home Alone is a single, honest ledger for all of it.

## What's inside

- **One ledger, every renewal** — streaming, AI tools, and home
  utilities (electricity, broadband, water, property tax) in a single
  chronological payment schedule with renewal countdowns.
- **Redundancy checks** — flags overlapping subscriptions doing the
  same job, and calculates annual-plan discount opportunities.
- **Sync without lock-in** — Supabase-backed multi-device sync, with a
  local-storage fallback so the app still works with no account
  configured, plus CSV export and JSON backup.

## Why it matters to me

A deliberately small, personal tool — light-mode by design, built for
five-minute check-ins rather than a finance dashboard you have to sit
down with. It's still under active development, currently running as a
household tool rather than a public product.
