## The brief

DUB Airspace began as a personal answer to a very local question: why is the
airport noisy today? Living close to Dublin Airport's north runway makes the
direction of departures matter as much as the aircraft themselves.

## The build

- **Airport briefing** — a concise arrivals and departures view, rather than
  a generic radar list.
- **Local context** — live Dublin METAR wind is translated into a likely
  eastbound or westbound operating pattern.
- **Animated airfield map** — ADS-B positions are replayed with a short delay
  so aircraft move smoothly across a simplified EIDW layout.
- **Phone-first PWA** — an installable personal tool, built to open quickly
  when the sound outside prompts the question.

## Why it matters

The interesting design problem was not showing more aviation data. It was
making operational context legible at a glance: wind, runway, direction and
the next movement all need to agree before the live map becomes useful.
