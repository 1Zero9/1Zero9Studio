## The idea

A personal portal revisiting a website concept from the 1990s — frame
layouts, multiple themes, the feel of an earlier web, rebuilt with today's
accessibility baseline rather than a straight nostalgia copy.

## Why it matters

Its own README says the quiet part out loud: the sample news, model
comparisons, and predictions are prototype content, not a verified live
service, and one of the two visitor counters is decorative while the other
is real and database-backed. That's an unusually honest content-status
disclosure to write into a README — worth carrying the same honesty
forward here rather than polishing it into sounding more finished than it
is.
