## The idea

Family paperwork — insurance renewals, NCT/car tax, medical letters,
utility bills — has a way of surviving as loose PDFs and photos nobody
files properly, until something is needed and can't be found. This
system is built to run for 10-20+ years: capture everything with zero
filing decisions at intake, then let AI do the organising work
afterwards.

## What's inside

Documents come in via drag-and-drop, mobile upload, or Gmail
auto-sync, get OCR'd with Tesseract, and are summarised by Claude for
dates, amounts, vendors, and document type. Fourteen category-specific
intelligence agents then generate proactive, cross-document insights —
"car insurance renewed but no payment found", "electricity bill jumped
40% versus last month" — and everything is scoped to specific people,
vehicles, pets, or properties rather than one undifferentiated pile.
A native macOS menu-bar app sits on top for status and quick actions.

## Why it matters to me

The most "in production" of the household tools — genuinely operational
since January 2026, with 500+ real documents ingested and running on a
Mac Mini rather than a dev laptop. It's also the most opinionated:
documentation is the source of truth for the system's behaviour, and
the core principle — zero decision-making at intake — is treated as
non-negotiable rather than aspirational.
