## The idea

Most "novel writing" software wants to be a production studio —
templates, publishing pipelines, community features. Bookworm is the
opposite bet: a calm, native macOS app built around the actual daily
work of writing a book, with everything else kept a click away rather
than on screen.

## What's inside

A continuous manuscript editor across chapters, a minimal Focus Mode
for when the interface needs to disappear, and an optional rendered
book preview alongside the draft. Planning and revision — World Bible,
storyboard, timeline, draft history, a "Red Pen" review pass — stay
available as separate workspaces rather than crowding the writing
surface. Manuscripts save as native `.bookworm` files with automatic
timestamped backups before every overwrite, and export to a portable,
chapter-by-chapter Markdown folder.

## Why it matters to me

A genuinely native app in a market that's mostly Electron wrappers —
built in Swift/SwiftUI on purpose, with real attention to the small
things: Keychain-backed credential storage, obsidian-dark and
warm-ivory themes designed for long sessions, and a restraint about
what the tool is allowed to interrupt you with.
