## The idea

Most family calendar apps assume one adult manages everything for
everyone else. Ground Control starts from a different premise: one
household login, but every member gets their own profile — so the
board reflects a family, not an admin and their dependents.

## What's inside

- **Mission control view** — a shared calendar and sticky-note board as
  the default landing surface for the day-to-day.
- **Modules, not monoliths** — Sports, School, Life, and Bills &
  Renewals are opt-in plug-ins for household-specific event types,
  rather than a fixed schema everyone has to fit into.
- **Profiles, not just accounts** — individual member views sit inside
  the one household login, so kids, partners, and admins can each see
  what's relevant to them.

## Why it matters to me

Same territory as JobJar and Home Alone — small, personal tools built
because the household actually needed them, not to chase a market. The
plug-in module approach is the interesting technical bit: most of
these apps calcify around whatever the first use case was, and I wanted
this one to grow sideways instead.
