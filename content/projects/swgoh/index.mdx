## The Brief

Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes (SWGOH) guild Territory Wars are high-stakes, 24-hour competitive events where 50 guild members coordinate defense zones and offense attacks against rival guilds. Guild officers traditionally run these wars out of chaotic spreadsheets and frantic Discord threads.

This platform unifies the entire SWGOH war effort into a single, real-time command console.

## What’s Inside the Platform

- **Interactive Territory War Battlefield** — A real-time, interactive canvas map of all defense sectors (North/South/Fleet zones) showing live banner counts, remaining enemy squads, and current zone targets.
- **Squad Counter Intelligence** — Instant counter recommendations against meta defensive walls (e.g. Galactic Legends, datacron variants, and off-meta traps), factoring in relic levels and turn meter speeds.
- **Real-Time Guild Sync** — Lightweight WebSocket synchronization ensuring all 50 guild members and officers see attacks, holds, and zone clears the instant they happen.
- **Discord Bot Automation** — Automatic match notifications, zone lock alerts, and attack command broadcasting directly to guild Discord channels.

## Engineering Highlights

The challenge was building an interface that handles rapid, concurrent inputs during the opening minutes of a war without lag or stale state. By leveraging HTML5 Canvas rendering alongside WebSocket event dispatching, the map updates smoothly at 60fps across desktop and mobile browsers.
