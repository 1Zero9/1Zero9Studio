## The idea

Workshop polling tools tend to be built for marketing sentiment, not
for a facilitator trying to get a room of stakeholders to an actual
decision. Audience is a proof of concept for the latter: collect
anonymous signal, let an AI co-pilot surface themes and split votes
with evidence, then generate a decision brief the room can act on.

## What's inside

A three-act loop — Collect (polls, ratings, word clouds via a join
code), Understand (a co-pilot panel analysing live responses for
themes and tensions), and Decide (a one-click session brief for
stakeholders) — plus a Discovery Workshop builder that can generate a
tailored interaction set from an agenda before a session even starts.
It runs entirely on localStorage, so a workshop needs no backend setup;
AI features work with built-in heuristics out of the box and get
sharper with an optional Anthropic or OpenAI key.

## Why it matters to me

A deliberately scrappy proof of concept — the interesting question
wasn't "can AI summarise workshop responses" (it can), it was whether a
facilitator would trust a one-click brief enough to actually walk into
a stakeholder meeting with it. Still a POC, not in regular use yet.
