## The idea

Every household's money moves through the same handful of things — bills,
income, accounts, transfers — but rarely lives in one place anyone can
actually check. Tally brings all of it into a single household ledger and
reconciles it against real bank statements, so a number on screen always
has a real record behind it.

## The build

Editorial, light-mode, and unapologetically calm for a finance app — no
dashboards trying to look busy. A real explainer page at `/about` (hero,
feature callouts, an actual screenshot of the app) sits in front of the
invite-only sign-in, so anyone who lands on the site — not just an invited
household member — understands what it is before being asked to sign in.

## Why it matters

Built for one household's real finances, not as a general SaaS product —
invite-only, no open signup, no advertising. The constraint that shaped it
most: every figure needs a trail back to a real bill, transfer or bank
statement line, not a number someone typed in and forgot to check.
