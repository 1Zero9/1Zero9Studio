## The brief

A public-facing site for a sports events business — the landing page, and
a contact flow that actually gets enquiries to a real inbox reliably.

## The build

A small brief on paper, but the contact pipeline got real hardening
anyway: reCAPTCHA and a honeypot field layered together rather than
trusting either alone, so the enquiry inbox stays usable instead of
drowning in bot submissions the moment the form goes public.

## Why it matters

The kind of detail that's invisible until someone floods a form with spam
— worth building in from day one rather than retrofitting after the first
bad week.
