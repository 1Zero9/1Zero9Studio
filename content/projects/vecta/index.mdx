## The idea

Job boards are noisy and ATS-scraped listings go stale fast. Vecta goes
directly to the source — real ATS feeds from the platforms companies
actually use — and layers a fit-scoring engine on top, specifically
tuned for AI/ML, cybersecurity, governance (GRC, NIS2, AI Act), and IT
infrastructure roles.

## What's inside

- **Vector Fit Engine** — scores each match out of 100 across skills,
  seniority caliber, and domain synergy, rather than a single vague
  percentage.
- **Application Copilot** — drafts a tailored cover letter and
  résumé bullets in the "accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z"
  format, plus a STAR-structured interview prep pack per role.
- **Company Radar** — an ecosystem directory tagging companies by scale
  tier and compliance badges (EU AI Act, ISO 42001, ISO 27001, NIS2,
  DORA, SOC 2).
- **Governance built in** — a GDPR Article 17 one-click data wipe and
  Article 20 data export, plus an EU AI Act Article 50/52 transparency
  statement, live in the product rather than bolted on for a compliance
  review.

## Why it matters to me

This is the same instinct as Astra applied to a different domain: take
a genuinely tedious, high-stakes process — job searching in a
regulation-heavy field — and make the AI's involvement legible and
auditable rather than a black box. Still an active build, currently
running on local-first storage with an optional Postgres upgrade path.
