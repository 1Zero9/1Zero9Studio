## The idea

Used-car shopping in Ireland means checking the same criteria across four
different sites by hand. Car Search set the criteria once and let Claude
do the searching.

## Why it matters

A real, working proof of concept — never version-controlled, and shelved
once the immediate need passed. Kept here as what it was: a genuinely
useful small tool, not a product taken further.
