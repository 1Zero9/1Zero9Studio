## The idea

Ireland doesn't get first-class treatment from most media-guide apps —
schedules default to US listings, streaming availability doesn't reflect
the Irish catalogue. Media Guide scopes every data source to Ireland
specifically: TV listings, streaming providers, and cinema releases all
pull from the IE region.

## Why it matters

A small, honest utility built to solve one real gap rather than to become
a platform — still actively evolving rather than a finished product.
